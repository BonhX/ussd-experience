Your services

Tap Shop to pay a merchant: pick one of your favorites by name or enter a new merchant code. The app builds the menu steps for you. Your DNI and PIN auto-fill from Settings → Profile. Every code is sent in the background through the phone's telephony service — the dialer and system USSD pop-ups are never shown. Replies, menus and confirmations all appear on the app's own screens.

Settings

👤 Profile
May start with zero.

Your DNI and PIN auto-fill the {dni} and {pin} fields of your codes (for example the wallet balance check). They are stored only on this device and the PIN is masked everywhere in the app.

📶 In-app USSD
Codes are sent with Android's TelephonyManager.sendUssdRequest(). The phone dialer and the system USSD dialog are never opened.
🤖 Auto-confirm from reply
Read the provider's reply and update the pending payment automatically when it reports success with a matching amount or reference, or a clear failure.

Requires Android 8.0+ and CALL_PHONE. READ_PHONE_STATE is used to enumerate SIM subscriptions when Detect SIMs is selected. Choose a SIM before sending to use that subscription; otherwise Android’s default SIM is used. Menus continue in-app using a chained code (e.g. *555# → *555*3#); no dialer is opened.

⭐ Favorite merchants

When you shop, favorites appear only by the name you gave them.

🛒 Shop payment route

Default for Tigo Money: *555 → 3 Pagos → 3 Pagar Comercio, then merchant code and amount. You only need to change this if your provider uses a different menu.

🌑 AMOLED mode
Pure black theme that saves battery on OLED screens.

Settings save automatically.

Danger zone

The app intercepts codes before they are sent, checks for scams such as call-forwarding tricks, and keeps a clean record of your shopping payments. All USSD traffic happens inside the app.
